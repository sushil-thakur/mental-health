# 🧠 Mental Well-being Hub 🌿

This repository hosts the source code for a dedicated platform focused on **mental health awareness, support, and resources**. Our aim is to create a welcoming and informative space to encourage open conversations and connect individuals with valuable tools for their well-being.

## ✨ A Sanctuary of Support

This website is crafted with care using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** to be accessible and user-friendly across all devices. We strive to provide a clean, empathetic, and reliable experience for everyone seeking information or assistance.

**Explore the Heart of Our Hub:**

* **Home (`home.html`):** Your starting point for navigating our resources and understanding our mission.
* **About Mental Health (`about.html`):** Delve into insightful information, dispel common misconceptions, and learn about the importance of mental well-being.
* **A Space to Share (`blog.html`):** Discover articles, personal narratives, and expert perspectives on a range of mental health topics.
* **Connecting with Support (`appointment.html`):** Explore pathways to connect with mental health professionals and support services.
* **Guidance from Professionals (`doctor.html`):** Find information about different types of mental health professionals and how they can help.
* **Your Virtual Companion (Chatbot - `video/`, `style.css`):** Engage with our interactive chatbot for initial guidance, answers to frequently asked questions, and links to further support.
* **Visual Harmony (`image/`, `video/`):** Thoughtfully selected media to enhance understanding and emotional connection.
* **A Calming Aesthetic (`style.css`):** Designed with a soothing color palette and intuitive layout to promote a sense of peace.
* **Interactive Elements (`script.js`, `app.js`):** Subtle animations and dynamic features to improve user engagement and accessibility.
